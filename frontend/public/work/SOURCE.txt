/work imagery — PLACEHOLDERS
============================
Every picture on the work index and the eight case study pages is a stock
stand-in. None of it is Creative Sphere work. It is here so a designer can read
what belongs in each slot; swap all of it for real project photography.

Previously this folder held the real client campaign key visuals recovered from
git (commit 01c7ce7^). They were replaced on request. They are not lost — they
are still in git history at that commit under public/hero/ and public/hero-06.webp.

Processing
----------
Crops chosen programmatically: slide a window across the photo, score each
position by edge energy, keep the busiest. A hand-picked first pass put two
crops on empty gradient. Sizes normalised to a per-role byte budget (banner
<= 260 KB, everything else <= 175 KB); esvolt's lead photo was swapped to the
installer shot because a dense aerial panel array is compression-hostile.

Copy
----
brief / approach / outcome in site.js were written from the one-line
descriptions and disciplines already on the site. Plausible, deliberately
non-specific, NOT client-approved. `stats` is null on seven of eight on
purpose — VFD is the only project with a figure the site already stands behind.
Do not fill the others in with invented numbers. sector, scope and the years
are also unconfirmed.
