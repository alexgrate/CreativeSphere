COPY — read before showing a client
-----------------------------------
`brief`, `approach` and `outcome` in site.js were written from the one-line
descriptions and the disciplines already on the site. They are plausible and
non-specific by design, but they are NOT client-approved and were not taken
from a brief. Have the team lead read all eight before this goes near a pitch.

`stats` is null on seven of the eight projects on purpose. VFD is the only
engagement with a figure the site already stands behind (~100 followers to
10,000+ in nine months, stated on the home page). The stats band simply does
not render when stats is null — do not fill the others in with invented
numbers to make the layout look even. Add them only when they are real.

`sector` and `scope` are reasonable but also unconfirmed.
