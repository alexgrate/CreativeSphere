PLACEHOLDER IMAGERY — what belongs in each slot
================================================
Every picture on the home and services pages used to be a client campaign key
visual, a product pack shot, or a screenshot of a client's Twitter profile with
the logo baked in. None of them described the slot they sat in, so there was no
way to read the design brief off the page.

They are now stand-ins chosen to show a designer WHAT KIND of image goes where.
Swap each for real Creative Sphere work. The studio photograph
(team/creativespace.webp) and the client logos in /logos are the real thing and
were left alone.
